# 📊 Mobile Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📝 Short Description / Purpose
This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of  mobile Phone's sales performance across India.  
It helps management track key metrics, identify top‑performing products and regions, and understand customer behavior for data‑driven decision‑making.

---

## ⚙️ Tech Stack
The dashboard was build by using following tools and technologies:
- **Data Source & Preparation:** Excel, SQL
- **File Format:** .pbix for development and .png for preview
- **Visualization & Dashboarding:** Power BI (interactive charts, maps, KPIs)  
- **Programming (optional):** Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)  
- **Business Intelligence Tools:** DAX (for measures/calculations in Power BI)

---

## 📂 Data Source
- **Primary Source:** Mobile sales transaction records (company database / Excel sheets)  
- **Data Fields Included:**  
  - Sales amount, quantity, and transaction IDs  
  - Brand and mobile model details  
  - Payment methods (UPI, Debit, Credit, Cash)  
  - Customer ratings (1–5 stars)  
  - Geographic information (city‑wise sales across India)  
  - Date and time of transactions (monthly and day‑wise analysis)

---

## ✨ Features / Highlights

### 1. Business Problem
Company needed a way to track mobile sales performance across India, identify top‑selling brands/models, and understand customer behavior (payment methods, ratings, purchase trends). Raw transaction data was scattered and difficult to interpret without visualization.

### 2. Goal of the Dashboard
To create a centralized, interactive dashboard that provides clear visibility into sales, transactions, customer ratings, and geographic distribution — enabling data‑driven decisions for marketing, inventory, and operations.

### 3. Walkthrough of Key Visuals
- **Top KPIs:** Total Sales (₹769M), Quantity (19K), Transactions (4K), Average Sales (₹40K)  
- **Trends:** Monthly quantity line graph, Day‑wise sales line graph (Saturday & Monday peaks)  
- **Geography:** Map of India showing city‑wise sales (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata as top contributors)  
- **Brand & Model Performance:** Apple and Samsung lead in sales (~₹160M each); iPhone SE, OnePlus Nord, Galaxy Note are top models  
- **Customer Behavior:** Payment split (Cards ~51%, UPI ~23%, Cash ~25%); Ratings skew positive but ~21.5% overall rating indicates improvement scope

### 4. Business Impact & Insights
- Identified **top‑performing brands and models** driving revenue  
- Pinpointed **peak sales days** (Saturday, Monday) for targeted promotions  
- Highlighted **strong markets** (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata) for regional strategy  
- Balanced payment preferences show **opportunity to boost UPI adoption**  
- Customer ratings analysis revealed **areas for service improvement**

---

## 📈 Impact
- Helped identify **top‑performing brands and cities**  
- Supported **marketing strategy** by highlighting peak sales days  
- Provided insights into **customer payment preferences**  
- Enabled management to focus on **high‑revenue models**
